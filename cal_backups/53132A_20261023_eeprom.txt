HP 53132A EEPROM backup

Instrument:   HEWLETT-PACKARD,53132A,0,4613  (*IDN?; firmware 4613; no serial number reported)
Part:         U14, AT28C64B, 8 KiB, mapped at 0x400000-0x401fff
Read:         2026-10-23, word by word with `@` through the counter's pForth console
              (:DIAG:SYST:PSTartup 1 and a power cycle; console on the rear RS-232,
              19200 8N1, null modem, via ser2net), 2048 reads of 4 bytes
File:         53132A_20261023_eeprom.bin, the 8192 bytes in address order
sha256:       4ac4852d9edab070ffcd95ae546e85117031754774c246667c94d5ded7e38a94

Holds the calibration and settings records (slots at 0x400020 and
0x400060, tag 0x5313).  The settings record carries the firmware tag
3944, from before the unit was upgraded to 4613.  The ROM read the same
way is byte for byte the published 4613 image.

Restore only with care: writing the EEPROM back replaces the calibration
and settings; see Jared Cabot's 531xxA Firmware Supplement.
